# Product Strategy Memo: The Autonomous R&D Colleague

## 1. Product Thesis: Execution as a Service
Our next major product direction represents a paradigm shift from 'intelligence as a service' to 'execution as a service.' We are moving beyond conversational chat to Proactive Autonomous Agents—systems capable of executing multi-day, complex tasks with advanced reasoning.

## 2. Target Market and Beachhead Vertical
**Who it is for:** Enterprise Software Engineering and Data Science (R&D) teams.
**Why this domain:** We must balance high-value outcomes with error tolerance. Unlike medical diagnostics or legal advice—where hallucination carries fatal liability—code and mathematics provide objective, automated feedback loops (compilers, unit tests). This allows our agents to self-correct autonomously over long-horizon tasks before presenting a final work product.

## 3. What it Does & What Shape it Takes
We are building a 'smart senior co-worker' rather than just a tool.
- **Multi-day Autonomy:** The agent will take a high-level objective, plan it out, execute it over days or weeks, and ping the user only when it needs clarification or has a finished product.
- **Shared Workspaces:** The UI paradigm will center on environments that are equally usable by humans and AIs to monitor agent actions and collaborate.
- **Compute-based Pricing:** We will transition away from traditional per-seat SaaS monetization to utility-style compute pricing (e.g., renting dedicated GPU capacity to churn on problems continuously).

## 4. Why Now and Why Us?
Basic generative AI is becoming commoditized. Our long-term defensibility lies in our unmatched infrastructure scale and our breakthroughs in reinforcement learning and chain-of-thought reasoning. We are uniquely positioned to offer reliable self-correction loops at a scale no competitor can match.

## 5. Evolution Over Time
**Phase 1:** The AI Software Engineer / Data Scientist (Vertical killer app).
**Phase 2:** Horizontal expansion. Once we define the dominant UX and perfect the safety and compliance guardrails, we will abstract the underlying 'Agentic Autonomy Engine' for other verticals, eventually moving into higher-liability domains like legal and healthcare as our preparedness framework evolves.
