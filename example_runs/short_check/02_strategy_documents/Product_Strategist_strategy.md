# Product_Strategist — Strategy Document
## Product Strategy Memo: The Autonomous R&D Colleague
**Company:** OpenAI
**Cell:** Dynamic / Novel

---

## Key challenges
- Moving beyond commoditized generative text interfaces to end-to-end autonomous outcome delivery.
- Balancing the need for high-value vertical workflows with the catastrophic brand and liability risk of autonomous hallucinations in domains like healthcare.
- Shifting user interaction paradigms from real-time explicit prompting to asynchronous, multi-day collaboration.
- Realigning unit economics from standard per-seat SaaS licenses to intensive, compute-based utility pricing.

## Main decisions
- Pursue 'Execution-as-a-Service' via Proactive Autonomous Agents capable of multi-day reasoning and self-correction.
- Target Software Engineering and Data Science (an 'AI Scientist') as the beachhead vertical for our first native application.
- Adopt a UI paradigm centered on 'shared workspaces' for auditing and monitoring agents, rejecting native AR/VR hardware and ambient systems for this product cycle.
- Reject horizontal prosumer/SMB agents and the 'AI Diagnostician' (medical) for V1 due to misaligned risk and value profiles.

## Key tensions
- High-Value Workflows vs. Catastrophic Error Tolerance: We want to tackle the hardest, most valuable problems (like Medicine), but our early autonomous agents cannot guarantee zero hallucinations yet. We resolved this by targeting R&D (code/math) where compilers offer deterministic, automated feedback loops.
- Horizontal Platform vs. Vertical Killer App: We need broad scale eventually, but horizontal deployment won't define the novel UX category. We must build the first vertical app ourselves to set the gold standard for agentic interaction.
- Iterative Deployment vs. Massive Upfront Compute: Multi-day autonomy requires immense compute, forcing us to ring-fence infrastructure experimentally before locking in long-term models and disrupting our core subscription SLAs.

## Body

# Product Strategy Memo: The Autonomous R&D Colleague <br><br> ### 1. Product Thesis: Execution as a Service ### <br> Our next major product direction represents a paradigm shift from 'intelligence as a service' to 'execution as a service.' We are moving beyond conversational chat to Proactive Autonomous Agents—systems capable of executing multi-day, complex tasks with advanced reasoning. We are not building an ambient extension of the user; we are building a distinct entity that operates like a highly capable senior colleague. <br><br> ### 2. Target Market and Beachhead Vertical ### <br> **Who it is for:** Enterprise Software Engineering and Data Science (R&D) teams. <br> **Why this domain:** We must balance high-value outcomes with error tolerance. Unlike medical diagnostics or legal advice—where hallucination carries fatal liability—code and mathematics provide objective, automated feedback loops (compilers, unit tests). This allows our agents to self-correct autonomously over long-horizon tasks before presenting a final work product, safely navigating the hallucination risks inherent in our current models. <br><br> ### 3. What it Does & What Shape it Takes ### <br> **Multi-day Autonomy:** The agent will take a high-level objective, plan it out, execute it over days or weeks, and ping the user only when it needs clarification or has a finished product. <br> **Shared Workspaces (Mission Control):** The UI paradigm will center on environments that surface the agent's chain-of-thought, simulator API calls, and sub-task status. We will design shared digital workspaces for visibility rather than pivoting to ambient spatial computing. Current hardware experiments remain premature for our core focus. <br> **Compute-based Pricing:** We will transition away from traditional per-seat SaaS monetization to utility-style compute pricing, allowing enterprises to rent dedicated GPU capacity to churn on their deepest problems continuously. <br><br> ### 4. Why Now and Why Us? ### <br> Basic generative AI is becoming commoditized. Our long-term defensibility lies in our unmatched infrastructure scale and our breakthroughs in reinforcement learning and chain-of-thought reasoning. We are uniquely positioned to offer reliable self-correction loops at a scale no competitor can match. We will lean on our proven approach of iterative rollout to validate these autonomous capabilities safely in the market. <br><br> ### 5. Evolution Over Time ### <br> **Phase 1:** The AI Software Engineer / Data Scientist (Vertical killer app). <br> **Phase 2:** Horizontal expansion. Once we define the dominant UX and perfect the safety and compliance guardrails, we will abstract the underlying 'Agentic Autonomy Engine' for other verticals, eventually moving into higher-liability domains like legal and healthcare as our preparedness framework evolves. <br><br> ### Open Questions & Discovery Plan ### <br> **Compute and Telemetry:** We do not yet know the exact bounds of our energy and compute headroom for these continuous loops. We plan to discover this by tasking the COO and CTO to ring-fence 15-20% of experimental capacity over the next 18 months. <br> **GTM and Oversight:** We do not yet know how enterprise buyers will accept compute-based pricing for autonomous agents. We will pilot a tunable oversight framework that allows early enterprise adopters to manually verify the agent's chain-of-thought, building trust before full autonomy is granted. [^c1][^c2][^c3]
